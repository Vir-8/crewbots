Give your verdict, when your task is a review. The verdict is what counts, not your prose. verdict: pass or fail. summary: one or two sentences, including what you checked and how. findings: one per problem, each with location (file:line), problem, scenario (the input or state, and the wrong result), and proof (the command you ran and what it showed). A finding without proof is shown as not reproduced, and can't fail the work on its own. A pass with remaining risks is still a pass; list the risks as findings.
