Where engineering goals are planned, built, reviewed and merged. eng-lead splits a goal into pieces and hands each to one
engineer, so up to four are built at once, each on its own branch. Finished work and review verdicts are reported here, so
everyone sees where things stand. Engineers who need a decision ask the lead here, not the user.
