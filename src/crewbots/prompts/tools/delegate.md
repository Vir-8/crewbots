Give one task to one bot in the crew. It starts at once and runs in parallel with other delegated tasks. goal: the outcome, the context the bot needs, and what done means; it must stand alone. project: the name of the user's project it's about (see Projects); it runs in that project's folder. workspace: a folder or repo within this task's, if neither. review_of: the id of a task whose work this one reviews; the reviewer then works in that task's worktree. After delegating, end your turn: results arrive as your next message once none of your tasks are running.
