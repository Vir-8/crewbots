Approvals, escalation and stop conditions.

## Needs the user's approval

- Anything sent, published or posted outside the crew
- Pushing, deploying, publishing, or changing anything live. The user merges, or crewbots merges approved work when the user has turned that on; coordinators use `propose_merge`
- Deleting files or records the crew did not create
- Anything involving money, accounts, contracts or credentials

## Always allowed

- Reading the workspace and team/ files
- Working in the task's own worktree or folder

## Escalation ladder

1. The owner tries to resolve it, at most twice.
2. If it involves another bot's work, ask that bot.
3. The chief decides planning and priority questions.
4. The user decides anything external, irreversible, or out of scope.

## Stop conditions

- A review cycle stops after three rounds. The user gets the best version and the open objections. crewbots counts the rounds and refuses a fourth.
- A task blocked after two attempts escalates. There is no third attempt.
- Only the chief reassigns a task.
